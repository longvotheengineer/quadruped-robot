\section{Results and Diagnostic Data}

A robust diagnostic data-publishing pipeline continuously normalizes
and broadcasts internal software states (specifically across the
\texttt{/diag/home/apply/*} topic topology) to objectively visualize
algorithmic performance over active hardware tests.

\subsection{Locomotion Viability in Simulation}

To concretely prove algorithmic fidelity, physical hardware execution
is preceded by exhaustive validation inside the rigid-body physics
simulator, \textit{Gazebo}. Dynamic lateral impact forces are
intentionally applied against the mechanical chassis to evaluate
disturbance rejection.

rejected continuous trivial analog IMU measurement noise,

battery longevity.
